## Logging Rides - The Easy Way

### Voice Logging (Recommended!)

This is the killer feature. While you're driving, just talk to your phone:

**Say something like:**

- _"Downtown to airport, UberX, twenty five dollars"_
- _"Pickup at mall, dropoff beach, thirty-two bucks, surge was two times"_
- _"Short ride to grocery store, UberX, eight dollars fifty"_

**The app understands:**

- **Locations** - "downtown", "airport", "123 Main St", "near the mall"
- **Ride types** - "UberX", "Uber XL", "Uber Black", "delivery"
- **Money** - "twenty five dollars", "$25", "25 bucks"
- **Surge** - "two times surge", "1.5x", "no surge"

### Manual Logging

If voice isn't working or you're somewhere quiet:

1. **Go to "Ride Logger"**
2. **Fill in the basics:**
   - Pickup location
   - Dropoff location
   - How much you earned
   - What type of ride
3. **Add extras if you want:**
   - Tips
   - Surge multiplier
   - How long it took
   - Notes (like "difficult passenger" or "construction detour")

### Pro Tips:

- **Log immediately** after each ride while it's fresh in your memory
- **Include tips** - they add up and you'll forget them
- **Note surge pricing** - this helps predict future surges
- **Voice logging works** even with road noise - just speak clearly

## Schedule - Your Crystal Ball

This is where the magic happens. The app predicts when and where surge pricing will hit.

### What You'll See:

- **Today's schedule** with surge predictions
- **High demand times** highlighted in colors
- **Recommended areas** to position yourself
- **Weather impact** on demand

### How to Use It:

1. **Check every morning** before you start driving
2. **Plan your breaks** around low-demand times
3. **Position yourself** in predicted surge areas 15-20 minutes early
4. **Set reminders** for peak times

### Reading the Colors:

- 🟢 **Green** = Normal demand, steady rides
- 🟡 **Yellow** = Increased demand, slightly higher fares
- 🟠 **Orange** = High demand, good surge potential
- 🔴 **Red** = Peak demand, maximum surge expected

### Pro Tips:

- **Airport runs** - check flight schedules, they're usually accurate
- **Events** - concerts, games, festivals create predictable surges
- **Weather** - rain and snow = instant surge
- **Rush hour** patterns repeat weekly

## Troubleshooting

### Voice Logging Not Working?

- **Check microphone permission** in your browser
- **Speak clearly** - road noise is okay
- **Use simple phrases** - "downtown to airport, $25"
- **Manual backup** - always available

### App Running Slow?

- **Close other browser tabs**
- **Restart your browser**
- **Check internet connection**
- **Clear browser cache** if problems persist

### Data Not Saving?

- **Stay logged in** - don't use private/incognito mode
- **Check internet** when logging rides
- **Export regularly** as backup

### Forgot Password?

- **Contact support** - we'll help you recover your account
- **Use the email** you signed up with
